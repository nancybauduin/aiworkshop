## Tables (partial, from first 500 lines of .df)

### Benefits (Area: Employee)
Employee benefits table.
- EmpNum (integer, PK via index "EmpNo")
- HealthCare (character x(8))
- LifeInsurance, Pension401K, StockPurchase, MedicalSpending, DependentCare (integer)

### BillTo (Area: Order)
Bill-to address information for an order.
- CustNum + BillToID (composite PK via index "custnumbillto")
- Name, Address, Address2, City, State, PostalCode, Contact, Phone

### Bin (Area: Inventory)
Warehouse bins containing items.
- BinNum (PK), Itemnum, WarehouseNum, Qty, BinName
- Indexes: BinNum (unique primary), ItemNum, WarehouseNumItemNum

### Customer (Area: Cust_Data)
Customer information including balance and address.
- CustNum (PK, validation: > 0)
- Name, Address, Address2, City, State, Country (default 'USA'), Phone, Contact
- SalesRep (validation: must exist in Salesrep)
- Comments, CreditLimit (0-9,999,999, default 1500), Balance
- Terms (default 'Net30'), Discount (>= 0), PostalCode, Fax
- Table triggers: CREATE (crcust.p), DELETE (delcust.p)

*Note: This is a summary of the first 500 lines of the schema. The full sports2000 schema contains additional tables (Item, Order, OrderLine, Salesrep, Warehouse, etc.).*
